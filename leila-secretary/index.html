💡 Leila's Top Tips for Germany

  • 🇩🇪 German employers value precision — always tailor each cover letter (Anschreiben) to the specific job.
  • 📄 A German CV (Lebenslauf) should include a professional photo, date of birth, and a clear chronological layout.
  • 🤝 The XING platform is Germany's LinkedIn — create a profile at xing.com.
  • 🗣️ Even basic German (A2–B1) significantly improves your chances. Consider a Goethe-Institut course.
  • ✅ Register your qualifications with the German ANABIN database if required for your field.
  • 📬 Always follow up applications after 1–2 weeks with a polite email.
