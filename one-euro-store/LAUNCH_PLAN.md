# €1 Store Launch Plan

## Business idea
Sell simple and affordable digital products priced around €1.

## Product groups
1. Games
2. Applications
3. Ebooks
4. AI Business Tools

## Recommended stack
- Website first
- Payments with Stripe or PayPal
- Store platform: Shopify or Lemon Squeezy

## First 10 product ideas
- Social media prompt pack
- Resume ebook
- Mini business planner
- Browser game template
- Budget calculator app
- Customer support chatbot prompts
- Email sales prompts
- Productivity checklist pack
- Startup guide ebook
- Digital invoice template
